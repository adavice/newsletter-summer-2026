EXPEDITION:VYTACHIV

OUR SUMMER ADVENTURE AWAITS

This summer, part of our team is heading to Vytachiv - a peaceful place where nature, fresh air and breathtaking views create the perfect setting to relax, reconnect and enjoy time together.

VYTACHIV, UKRAINE

Located on the banks of the Dnipro River, Vytachiv is one of Ukraine's hidden gems, known for its scenic landscapes and unforgettable sunsets.

Whether it's sharing stories over dinner, watching the sunset or simply enjoying each other's company, this expedition is about creating memories that will stay with us long after summer ends.

DID YOU KNOW?

One of the oldest settlements in the Kyiv region.

Famous panoramic views over the Dnipro River.

A popular destination for photographers.

A perfect place to disconnect from routine.
